1. Go to System → Accounts → Accounts
  2. Click the absence account A300 (and later Z302)
  3. Click Link posting in the top right
  4. Click the blue plus to add a new Link posting
  5. Select the corresponding balance account (X300) as the Link posting account
  6. Put in "-1" or "1" as the Factor for the Absence account and Transfer to next year account respectively.
